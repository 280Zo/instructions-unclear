# Evaluating Source Quality

Before deep-reading, filter sources with no verifiable work, misaligned commercial incentives, circular credentials, positive-only advice, or evidence that the author is no longer actively practicing.

Prefer practitioners with shipped products, open-source contributions, measurable case studies, or first-hand accounts over commentators producing roundups without original work. For credibility-sensitive tasks, verify recommendations, track record, and criticism with separate searches.

For each source, return a short observed `quality` description, for example `shipped the product; direct experience` or `roundup blog; no original work shown`. Do not force sources into fixed quality categories.
