# Superpowers

These apply when the Superpowers plugin is installed.

* Never stage or commit anything in `docs/superpowers/`. Superpowers' skills say to commit their specs and plans there; these are working files and stay out of git.
* Superpowers' other commit steps (plan steps, finishing a branch) follow the commit rule in "Scope, Secrets and Approvals": ask first, showing the command, message, files and changes.
